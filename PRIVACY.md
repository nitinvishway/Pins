# Privacy Policy for PinVault

This privacy policy explains how **PinVault** (the "Extension") handles user information.

## 1. No Collection of Personal Information
PinVault does **not** collect, store, or transmit any personal data, usage metrics, or browsing history. The extension does not communicate with any external servers, and we do not use any analytics tools or tracking scripts.

## 2. Information Access and Local Processing
To perform its bulk downloading functionality, the Extension accesses:
*   **Active Tab Content (Pinterest DOM)**: Read locally in your browser to identify Pin images on the boards you navigate to.
*   **Chrome Downloads API**: Used locally to save your selected images directly to your device's default `Downloads` directory.

All processing occurs entirely within your local browser sandbox. No data is ever shared, sold, or transmitted to the developer or any third-party services.

## 3. Data Control and Deletion
Since no data is collected or stored on external servers, you have full control over the extension's files locally. Removing the extension from your browser instantly deletes all extension-related configurations.

## 4. Contact
For questions or support, you can open an issue on our GitHub repository: https://github.com/nitinvishway/Pins.
